### **Pillars for Building AI-Native Services:**

* **Picking the Right Market (**1:01** - **3:43**):**
  Focus on markets with low trust (outsourced work), low judgment
  requirements at the task level, high intelligence thresholds for the
  total outcome, and regulated environments which can create a "moat."
  Avoid areas involving heavy physical equipment or on-site labor.
* **The Founding Team (**4:35** - **5:28**):** Successful founders need a blend of  **domain fluency** , **model fluency** (understanding what AI can do), and **operational rigor** (managing throughput and cycle times).
* **Building the Product (**5:28** - **6:46**):** Treat the process as the product. Focus on **reducing variance** in outputs, as inconsistency destroys trust. Ensure humans in the loop scale non-linearly with the technology.
* **Sales and Pricing (**6:46** - **8:41**):** Avoid the "early demand trap" where too many pilots overwhelm your ability to build. Sell  **outcomes** , not seats or tokens, and use per-unit or outcome-based pricing rather than cost-plus models.
* **The P&L Math (**8:41** - **10:27**):** Obsess over **COGS**
  (model costs, hosting, and human labor). The ultimate goal is to
  achieve "AI operating leverage" to move margins closer to software
  levels (50%+) rather than traditional service firm levels (30%).

### **Crucial Warning**

* **Don't Buy Your Way In (**10:27** - **11:10**):** *Warren*
  strongly advises against buying legacy services businesses to "add AI
  on top," as legacy metrics and cultures are often incompatible with the
  required AI-native operational shifts.
